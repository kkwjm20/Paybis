**What People Complain About:**

The fees are higher than some competitors. If you're the type who obsessively compares prices down to the cent, platforms like Binance offer lower fees - but you're trading convenience for cost savings there. Binance also makes you wait days for bank transfers and navigate an interface that looks like a spaceship control panel.

Some users get hit with verification requests after their first few transactions. This frustrates people who thought they were done with that step. Paybis's response? It's required by financial regulations when you hit certain transaction thresholds. Not fun, but not exactly unique to Paybis.

A handful of reviews mention declined transactions with no clear explanation. When this happens, it's usually your bank blocking the payment (because many banks still treat crypto like it's radioactive), but occasionally it's Paybis's security system being overly cautious.

The mobile app gets mixed feedback. Some people love the convenience. Others report login issues and occasional glitches with verification codes.

**The Security Story:**

Paybis is registered with FinCEN in the US (registration No. 31000197902174) and FINTRAC in Canada. They use Multi-Party Computation (MPC) technology to split private keys across multiple servers, which is fancy talk for "your crypto is harder to steal."

They enforce strict KYC (Know Your Customer) verification. Some people hate this because it feels invasive. Others appreciate it because it means the platform is actually following regulations and trying not to get shut down by governments.

Two-factor authentication is available (though not as robust as some exchanges). Most customer funds are in cold storage - meaning offline where hackers can't easily reach them.

**The Bottom Line from Real Users:**

Most people are satisfied. The platform does what it says it'll do: get you crypto quickly without making you feel like you need a degree in blockchain technology. The fees are higher than some alternatives, but you're paying for speed and simplicity.

Is it perfect? No. Will it work for most people who just want to buy some Bitcoin without overthinking it? Yes.
